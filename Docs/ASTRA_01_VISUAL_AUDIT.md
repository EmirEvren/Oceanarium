# ASTRA 0.1 — pre-change visual audit

Inspected OF_HomeIsland in the running Unity 6000.3.13f1 editor, Play Mode, Full HD 1920x1080 Game view, 2026-10-16.

- Broad flat center lacks elevation and a focal composition.
- Paths have hard angular elbows and abrupt material edges.
- Ground grain repeats visibly, creating a patterned carpet appearance.
- Saturated lime fronds and grass disagree with darker opaque water.
- Palms repeat the same thin striped trunk and sparse crown silhouette.
- Ferns resemble star-shaped flat leaves; rocks resemble smooth ellipsoids.
- Cabin has thin slab walls/roof, little framing depth, and bare porch.
- Aquarium reserve is empty; spawn has little deliberate composition.
- Horizon water appears flat and dark; shore needs depth-based contact foam.
- Existing character is readable but hands disconnect visually from short sleeves.
- Existing camera follows correctly at spawn; obstacle and movement regressions will be rerun after changes.

ASTRA 0.0 saved test evidence already reports passing W, sprint, mouse orbit, recovery, rod pickup and transitions. This is historical evidence, not a new 0.1 test run.
